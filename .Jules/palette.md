## 2025-05-15 - Visually de-emphasize completed tasks
**Learning:** Completed tasks should be visually de-emphasized (e.g., via graying out) to reduce cognitive load and help users focus on remaining work. In Tkinter's Treeview, tag precedence is determined by the order of tags in the item's tag sequence, and colors must be explicitly re-configured during theme toggles to maintain accessibility.
**Action:** Always provide a visual distinction for completed states and ensure color contrast remains WCAG AA compliant across all supported themes (Light/Dark mode).
