## 2026-07-15 - Visual Hierarchy of Completed Tasks
**Learning:** Using a de-emphasized color (grayed out) for completed tasks significantly improves the visual hierarchy of the task list, enabling users to maintain focus on pending items while keeping a satisfying record of their accomplishments. To satisfy WCAG AA accessibility contrast ratios (at least 4.5:1), we must select the de-emphasized foreground colors carefully depending on the active theme: foreground `#595959` on background `#f8f9fa` (contrast ~6.6:1) in light mode, and foreground `#a9a9a9` on background `#212529` (contrast ~6.4:1) in dark mode.
**Action:** Always apply the completed/done style tag first in the Treeview tags tuple to override high/medium/low priority backgrounds, and explicitly configure both matching background and accessible foreground colors dynamically during theme toggle operations.
