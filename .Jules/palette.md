## 2026-07-15 - Visual Hierarchy of Completed Tasks
**Learning:** Graying out and de-emphasizing completed tasks dramatically improves the visual hierarchy of the task list, making active tasks stand out clearly.
**Action:** Prepend the 'done' tag in ttk.Treeview before any priority-based tag to ensure visual precedence, and configure background/foreground colors explicitly to meet WCAG AA contrast standards.
