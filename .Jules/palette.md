## 2025-05-14 - Visually de-emphasize completed tasks
**Learning:** Completed tasks should be visually distinguished from active ones to reduce cognitive load. Muting their color helps users focus on remaining work while still maintaining a record of accomplishment.
**Action:** Use a "done" tag in the Treeview to apply a grayed-out style (foreground color) and neutral background to completed tasks. Ensure colors meet WCAG AA contrast standards in both light and dark modes.
