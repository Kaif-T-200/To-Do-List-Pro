## 2026-07-15 - Visual Hierarchy of Completed Tasks
**Learning:** Using a de-emphasized color (grayed out) for completed tasks improves the visual hierarchy of the task list, allowing users to focus on active tasks while maintaining visibility of accomplishments. When applying overlapping priority backgrounds and done statuses, the 'done' tag must be prioritized and explicitly define a neutral background matching the app's theme to maintain a minimum of 4.5:1 (WCAG AA) contrast with the de-emphasized foreground text.
**Action:** In `ttk.Treeview`, prepend or place the status/override tag (e.g., 'done') first in the tags tuple and configure both the foreground (dark gray/light gray) and background (to match theme background) explicitly to avoid readability issues and maintain compliance across light and dark modes.
