## 2026-07-09 - Visual De-emphasis of Completed Tasks
**Learning:** Using a de-emphasized color (grayed out) for completed tasks improves the visual hierarchy of the task list. It allows users to focus on active tasks while maintaining visibility of their accomplishments. To maintain WCAG AA compliance, a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 must be preserved (e.g., #595959 on #f8f9fa for light mode, #a9a9a9 on #212529 for dark mode).
**Action:** Always apply a status-based style override to completed items that provides clear visual feedback of completion without sacrificing readability.
