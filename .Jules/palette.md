## 2025-05-14 - Visual De-emphasis for Completed Tasks
**Learning:** In list-based UIs with priority-based coloring, completed items can create visual clutter if they retain their high-priority background colors. De-emphasizing them with a neutral foreground and matching background (effectively removing the priority color) improves focus on remaining tasks.
**Action:** Use a 'done' tag that appears last in the Treeview tags tuple to override priority colors, and ensure WCAG AA compliance for the de-emphasized text in both light and dark modes.
