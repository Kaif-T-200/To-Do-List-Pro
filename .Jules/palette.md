# Palette Journal

## 2025-05-15 - De-emphasizing completed tasks with accessible contrast
**Learning:** When using colors to indicate status (like priority), completed tasks can become visually overwhelming if they retain those colors. De-emphasizing them with a gray foreground and a neutral background improves focus on active tasks. To maintain WCAG AA compliance, a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 (standard) or 7:1 (high) should be targeted. In this app, #595959 on #f8f9fa (light mode, ~7:1) and #a9a9a9 on #212529 (dark mode, ~4.5:1) were used.
**Action:** Always verify contrast ratios for de-emphasized text and ensure the 'done' tag is applied last in Treeview to override priority-based background colors.
