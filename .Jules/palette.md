## 2024-05-23 - Visual Hierarchy for Completed Tasks
**Learning:** Using a de-emphasized color (grayed out) for completed tasks improves the visual hierarchy of the task list, allowing users to focus on active tasks while maintaining visibility of accomplishments. To maintain WCAG AA compliance, the foreground color must have a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against the background.
**Action:** Implement a 'done' tag in the task list that applies a neutral gray foreground and resets the background to the app's neutral background, ensuring it takes precedence over priority-based highlighting.
