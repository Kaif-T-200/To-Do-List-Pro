## 2026-06-29 - Visual Hierarchy for Completed Tasks
**Learning:** Completed tasks should be de-emphasized to reduce cognitive load, but must maintain WCAG AA contrast (at least 4.5:1) against their background to remain accessible. Overlapping priority colors with a status-based neutral color helps users focus on remaining work.
**Action:** When implementing status updates in list views, use de-saturation or neutral tones for completed items and ensure theme-aware color configurations are applied during the refresh cycle.
