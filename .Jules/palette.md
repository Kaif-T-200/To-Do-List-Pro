## 2024-05-22 - Visual Hierarchy for Completed Tasks
**Learning:** Using a de-emphasized color (grayed out) for completed tasks improves the visual hierarchy of the task list, allowing users to focus on active tasks while maintaining visibility of accomplishments. To maintain WCAG AA compliance, the foreground color must be carefully selected against the background (e.g., #595959 on #f8f9fa in light mode, #a9a9a9 on #212529 in dark mode).
**Action:** Implement a 'done' tag in the task list that overrides priority-based background colors and applies a de-emphasized foreground color.
