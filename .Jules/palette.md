## 2026-07-15 - Visual Hierarchy of Completed Tasks
**Learning:** Using a de-emphasized color (grayed out) for completed tasks improves visual hierarchy by allowing users to focus on active tasks while maintaining visibility of accomplishments. Prepending the 'done' tag first in the tags list/tuple ensures visual precedence over priority-based background colors in `ttk.Treeview`. To maintain WCAG AA compliance (contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1), the foreground color must be `#595959` on neutral light background `#f8f9fa` (contrast ~6.6:1) and `#a9a9a9` on dark mode background `#212529` (contrast ~6.4:1).
**Action:** Prepend the status override tag (e.g., 'done') first in the tags sequence to override other overlapping visual elements. Explicitly configure neutral light and dark background and foreground colors for the 'done' tag once in a theme-updating method rather than in high-frequency loops, and update text cursor colors explicitly to white in dark mode.
