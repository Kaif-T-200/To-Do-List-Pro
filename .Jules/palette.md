## 2026-07-15 - Visual Hierarchy of Completed Tasks
**Learning:** Using a de-emphasized color (grayed out) for completed tasks improves the visual hierarchy of the task list, allowing users to focus on active tasks while maintaining visibility of accomplishments. To maintain WCAG AA compliance with background color differences, the de-emphasized completed task styles must be defined explicitly with appropriate foreground and background colors in both light and dark modes (using foreground `#595959` on background `#f8f9fa` in light mode, and foreground `#a9a9a9` on background `#212529` in dark mode).
**Action:** When styling a `ttk.Treeview`, configure priority-based tags alongside a 'done' tag. Apply the 'done' tag first in the tags tuple so that its visual styles take precedence over the priority background colors. Perform tag configurations in a theme update method to avoid high-frequency overhead in refresh loops.
